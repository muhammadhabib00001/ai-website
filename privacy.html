Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Revised: September 1, 2026 • Effective for all global readers

1. Overview & Commitment to Reader Privacy

NexusSphere Media Inc. ("NexusSphere", "we", "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and digital sovereignty of our readers. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data practices, explaining what minimal information we collect, how it is secured, and your rights under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization. We only collect information strictly required to deliver our analytical publications:

  • Voluntary Newsletter Subscriptions: Your email address when you voluntarily opt into our weekly intelligence dispatches.
  • Editorial Inquiries: Name, contact details, and institutional affiliation provided via our manuscript and press contact forms.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Privacy-preserving aggregated traffic metrics (page visits, device viewport sizes) without storing personally identifiable information or third-party tracking pixels.

3. How We Use Information

We never sell, rent, or monetize reader data to third-party advertising brokers. Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver requested editorial newsletters and research briefs.
  • Respond to reader inquiries, manuscript submissions, and press requests.
  • Optimize the performance, accessibility, and speed of our web application.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

NexusSphere uses modern HTML5 localStorage solely to remember your chosen theme preference (Light or Dark mode). We do not deploy cross-site tracking cookies or intrusive advertising telemetry.

5. Terms of Service & Intellectual Property

All original journalism, visual diagrams, editorial photography, and proprietary data tables published on NexusSphere are protected under international copyright law. Educational quotations and fair-use citations are permitted with direct attribution and canonical linking to the original article URI.

6. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For inquiries regarding your data rights or to request deletion of your newsletter subscription record, email our compliance team at privacy@nexussphere.io.
